03 / CAPTURE PIPELINE

Import a scan

This browser only

Select your faction above before importing. Imports are kept locally in this browser's IndexedDB, not shared across players or sent to a server.

Supported: JSON with scanTime and items[], or CSV with item_id,item_name,buyout_copper,stack_size,scan_time. A real scan timestamp is required. This imports a normalized scan—not native Auctionator .lua yet.

For accurate prices, a buyout must be the total price of its stack; the system converts to a per-unit price. Native Auctionator decoding and authenticated shared uploads remain under development.
