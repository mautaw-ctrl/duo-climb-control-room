SOURCES & METHOD

Know what you’re reading.

The spellbook covers documented new class abilities and major changes versus Classic Era, reconciled against beta updates through November 2. “New” means new to Forever versus Classic; “Baseline” means a previous talent or racial is now generally available. Removed beta abilities are kept separately. Unchanged Classic spells, rank-by-rank values, minor talent moves and discovered scroll variants are outside this overview. PvP and PvE paragraphs are editorial hypotheses based on toolkit and role, with low confidence until launch data is available.
