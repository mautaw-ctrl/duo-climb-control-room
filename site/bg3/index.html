04 · Easy combat plan

  1. Stay near your frontline, shield equipped.
  2. Open with Bless or a useful damage spell.
  3. Use Bone Chill on enemies when conserving spell slots.
  4. At Cleric 2, Touch of Death adds necrotic damage to a melee hit.
  5. Use Healing Word to rescue a downed teammate, not to top off healthy allies.

Only one concentration spell can be active at a time.
